What is Garbhsanskar

The ancient science of nurturing before birth

Garbhsanskar means education of the womb — the practice of consciously shaping a child's character, intellect and temperament while they are still developing inside the mother. Rooted in ancient wisdom and supported by what we now understand about prenatal development, it holds that a mother's thoughts, emotions, sounds and routines are felt by her baby.

Through structured daily practice — prayer, positive reading, gentle movement, breathwork and calm reflection — a mother creates an inner environment of peace and purpose. This is how Garbhsanskar helps a mother give shape to the child she envisions: calm, intelligent, compassionate and strong.
